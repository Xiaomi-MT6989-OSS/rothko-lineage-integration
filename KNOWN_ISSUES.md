# Known issues and validation limits

LC3 / LE Audio is the open audio issue reported for LineageOS 24.0. A prior
single-headset test established playback in both earbuds. Media stream
selection and switching between devices still need targeted acceptance.

Additional test coverage remains pending for SIM-free automatic time zone,
China Telecom/China Broadnet carrier scenarios, longer AOD operation,
ambient white balance, specialized camera HDR/NFC and some Google services.
See [VALIDATION.md](VALIDATION.md) for the distinction between checks on the
November 19 local release and checks on this public source snapshot.
